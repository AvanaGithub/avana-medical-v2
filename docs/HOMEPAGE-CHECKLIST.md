# Homepage checklist

What a B2B medical-device homepage should have, checked against the Avana Medical homepage on 15 Oct 2026.

Key: ✅ in place · ⚠️ partly in place · ❌ missing

## 1. First screen (above the fold)

| | Item | Our homepage |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ | Logo, top-left, linking to home | In place |
| ✅ | Clear menu: About, Products, Medical Education, Careers, Contact | In place, with a mobile menu |
| ✅ | A headline that says **what Avana does and for whom** | "Orthopaedic and sports medicine technology for surgeons across India" above the product of the month (added 15 Oct) |
| ✅ | A main call to action in the hero, e.g. "Explore products" or "Contact our team" | "Explore the Arthrex range" and "Talk to our team" (added 15 Oct) |
| ✅ | Strong visual: video or image | Hero video, with a pause button |
