Market leader in joint preservation, with a growing presence across orthopaedic specialties.

Through Avana Medical, Avana Surgical and Avana Technologies, we bring arthroscopy, arthroplasty, spine and capital equipment to hospitals across India and the SAARC region, backed by clinical support in every case.

MovMedix Product Catalogue

MovMedix, the maker of the LARS (Ligament Advanced Reinforcement System) ligament, is based near Dijon, France. Its knitted PET (polyethylene terephthalate) prostheses are used to reconstruct and reinforce ligaments and tendons. All are sterile and supplied in a range of sizes.
