S. Singaravel

He takes ownership of every service commitment and builds a team that solves problems with transparency and a drive for continuous improvement.

MovMedix Product Catalogue

MovMedix, the maker of the LARS (Ligament Advanced Reinforcement System) ligament, is based near Dijon, France. Its knitted PET (polyethylene terephthalate) prostheses are used to reconstruct and reinforce ligaments and tendons. All are sterile and supplied in a range of sizes.
